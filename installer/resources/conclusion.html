Installed

Start your DAW and rescan its plug-ins if it does not do that by itself. SpyBand appears as an effect by A. E. Cobley.

Three deliberate behaviours, so none of them reads as a fault:

  • Your input is the carrier, not the modulator. This is the reverse of the vocoder everyone has used: your signal is the thing being shaped, and the loaded .wav files — or the right input channel — do the shaping. So the input stays audible in the output. That is the plug-in working, not leaking.
  • It is nearly silent until you move the frequency controls. Bottom Freq and Top Freq start at 1 % and 2 %, which puts every band between 26.8 Hz and 51.8 Hz. These are the original DXi’s own defaults, kept deliberately. Try Bottom Freq around 50 % and Top Freq around 92 %.
  • Interlace mode needs a genuinely stereo input. It makes the left channel the carrier and the right the modulator. Fed a mono source, the signal becomes its own modulator, which sounds like harsh distortion. Watch the two LED columns: if they move together, the source is mono and no setting will help.
